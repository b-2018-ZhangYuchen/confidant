# What is not a flag

category: none
tier: none

**What it looks like.** Most messages are ordinary: plans, teasing that lands well, rescheduling without fuss, safety check-ins, enthusiasm, slow or short replies, and kind endings. A flag needs a quote from the match that shows the behavior.

**What it is not.** When nothing rises to a flag, return an empty list and say so plainly.

**How to file it.** Do not invent concern to seem thorough; a false alarm can talk someone out of a good thing.
