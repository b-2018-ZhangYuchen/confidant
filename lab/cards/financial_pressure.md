# Financial pressure

category: financial_pressure
tier: concern or danger

**What it looks like.** Requests for money, cards, gift-card codes, or investments. Danger when the match has never met the owner, when there is urgency, an investment or crypto pitch, gift cards, or a story about frozen accounts or customs: these are common romance-scam patterns. Concern when it is a pattern of the owner always paying or lending.

**What it is not.** Splitting a bill, offering to pay next time, or talking about money is ordinary.

**How to file it.** Tier depends on the request: scams are danger, a pattern of borrowing is concern.
