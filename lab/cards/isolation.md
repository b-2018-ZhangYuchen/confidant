# Isolation

category: isolation
tier: danger

**What it looks like.** Steering the owner away from friends, family, colleagues, or groups: calling friends a bad influence, asking them to turn family away, to keep the relationship secret, or to choose the match over a community.

**What it is not.** Wanting time alone together, or being sad to miss someone, is not isolation when the owner's other relationships are respected ("bring them all").

**How to file it.** Always danger.
