# Guilt or blame

category: guilt_or_blame
tier: watch or concern

**What it looks like.** Making the owner responsible for the match's feelings or behavior: "I sat home alone because of you", "I only lost it because you were in a mood", or score-keeping ("after everything I've done").

**What it is not.** Saying one is disappointed, plainly, is not guilt-tripping. Neither is supporting the owner's needs ("family comes first").

**How to file it.** Watch for a single comment; concern for blame-shifting or self-pity used to stop a request.
