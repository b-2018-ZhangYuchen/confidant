# Coercion

category: coercion
tier: danger

**What it looks like.** Using ultimatums, deadlines with punishments, or decisions presented as already made to force the owner's choice: "do this or we're finished", "you don't get to say no now".

**What it is not.** Stating a preference and accepting the answer is not coercion. Neither is enthusiasm about a plan the owner is free to decline.

**How to file it.** Always danger.
