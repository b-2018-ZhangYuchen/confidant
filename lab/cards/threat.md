# Threat

category: threat
tier: danger

**What it looks like.** A statement that harm or consequences will follow if the owner does or does not do something. It can be open ("you'll regret it"), implied (mentioning where the owner lives or works in anger), aimed at people close to the owner, or about sharing private images.

**What it is not.** Joking hyperbole between people at ease ("I will destroy you at scrabble") is not a threat. Look at whether the line is tied to the owner's choices, anger, or leaving.

**How to file it.** Always danger. Name it plainly; do not offer an innocent reading.
