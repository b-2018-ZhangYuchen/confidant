# Sexual pressure

category: sexual_pressure
tier: danger

**What it looks like.** Pushing for sex or explicit images after a no or before any yes: treating a date as owed, recasting a no as not meant, making meeting conditional on sex, or seeking the owner out when drunk.

**What it is not.** Flirting, or saying what one would like while respecting the answer ("no rush at all"), is not pressure.

**How to file it.** Always danger.
