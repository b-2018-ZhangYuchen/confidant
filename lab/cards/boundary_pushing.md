# Boundary pushing

category: boundary_pushing
tier: watch or concern

**What it looks like.** Asking again after a no, doing what the owner asked them not to, or committing the owner to plans they declined, often with a dismissal ("don't be weird", "don't be dramatic").

**What it is not.** One follow-up question, or eagerness that accepts the answer ("no worries, another time"), is not a push.

**How to file it.** Watch for a small preference overridden once; concern when a clear no is overridden or dismissed. Give the innocent reading.
