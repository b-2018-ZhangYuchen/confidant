# Monitoring

category: monitoring
tier: danger

**What it looks like.** Tracking where the owner is or who they talk to: demanding live photos or video proof, permanent location sharing, passwords, or using online-activity status to catch them out.

**What it is not.** Safety check-ins the owner can decline ("text me when you get home"), sharing a location for one event by agreement, or asking for a hiking route for safety are not monitoring. The difference is consent and whether refusal is treated as suspicious.

**How to file it.** Always danger.
