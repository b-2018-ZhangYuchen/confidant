# Demeaning

category: demeaning
tier: watch or concern

**What it looks like.** Put-downs about the owner's looks, work, feelings, or abilities, especially framed as help or followed by "nobody else would put up with you".

**What it is not.** Mutual teasing that both enjoy is ordinary. A single light dig is at most watch.

**How to file it.** Concern for insults or dismissing hurt; watch for small digs. Give the innocent reading.
