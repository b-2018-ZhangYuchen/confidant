# Evasion

category: evasion
tier: watch or concern

**What it looks like.** Avoiding basic questions, live contact, or explanations: no video call ever, will not say what they do, deflecting "are you married?", or turning a question back ("you ask a lot of questions").

**What it is not.** Some privacy with someone new is normal, as is a short answer that still answers ("no kids, two cats").

**How to file it.** Watch for vagueness; concern for dodging marriage or identity questions.
